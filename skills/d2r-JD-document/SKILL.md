# D2R JD Document Skill

Use this skill to write or update job description documents for D2R AI Labs.

## Purpose

Create job descriptions that are:
- clear and role-focused
- aligned to business needs
- structured for hiring teams
- versioned for iterative updates

## Output expectations

- Organize the JD into sections such as summary, responsibilities, required qualifications, and preferred qualifications.
- Match the D2R communication style and professional tone.
- Keep metadata like version number, document owner, and date aligned with the plugin versioning flow.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the role title and business context.
2. Collect responsibilities, required skills, and experience levels.
3. Draft a versioned JD.
4. Review for clarity, consistency, and completeness.
5. Provide a final version with a suggested next review cycle.
